Screenshot parsing

Paste or drop screenshots, give each one a category (or leave Auto-detect), then read them. Nothing is added until you have reviewed the result and pressed Apply. Reading uses Claude and is limited to the owner of this page.

Paste screenshots here Press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) anywhere on this page, drop image files, or click to choose them.

If a team name is visible on a roster, picks or stats screen, the rows go to that team: an existing one is matched by name, and an unknown name creates a new team. With no name visible they go to the open team. Rows from a roster screenshot are tagged check until you edit them. Claude can misread a digit, so compare against the screen. If a rating shows a ▲ or ▼ change, the League tab setting decides whether it is undone to get the base rating.

Trade parser

Paste the text of a trade, for example from your league chat. Claude reads who sends what to whom, the page matches it to your rosters and picks, checks it against the salary rule and the first-round pick rule, and shows the result. You then apply it, which moves the players and picks.

Activity log

Every screenshot parse, trade, Excel import and undo is recorded with its date. Undo restores the data from before the last apply on this page. It is kept in memory only, so it is gone after a reload.

Rotation checker

Enter minutes and tick the starters. The checks use the rule values on the League tab, which start from what you described in your league chat (minute floors for the top players, sub-70 limits, bench depth, one position off).

    Standings

    Enter each team's record, or paste a standings screenshot on the Parsing tab. The teams with the worst records go into the draft lottery. Lottery size, picks drawn and the odds are on the League tab.

    Draft lottery

    The default odds and the four-pick draw are the current NBA format, from the Hoops Rumors lottery glossary, accessed 27 Oct 2026. Your sim league may use another format, so edit them on the League tab. Ties here are broken by more losses, then by team name, not by the real tiebreaker. Odds are computed exactly by working through every possible draw.

    League audit

    Checks every team's saved rotation against the anti-tanking rules on the League tab. A team shows "no rotation" until its rotation has been entered on the Rotation tab (open that team first).

    League settings and rules

    Cap, tax line and apron defaults are the real NBA 2019-20 figures from Hoops Rumors, accessed 27 Oct 2026. Your league year and cap can differ, so edit them to match your league. The salary floor (90% of the cap) and the prospect and veteran age lines (23 and 29) come from my own judgement or memory and are not sourced. The trade kicker is 25% for a team under the cap and 0% for a team at or over it, which is my reading of what you told me. Change either value above if your league works differently. Treat them as placeholders. The rotation rule values come from your league chat: 240 minutes, top three at 28 or more, top two start, sub-70 players capped at 10 minutes, bench of 12. The chat did not say exactly how bench depth is counted, so I count bench players with minutes.
