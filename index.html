    League settings and rules

    Cap, tax line and apron defaults are the real NBA 2019-20 figures from Hoops Rumors, accessed 27 Oct 2026. Your league year and cap can differ, so edit them to match your league. The salary floor (90% of the cap), the trade matching rule (125% of outgoing salary plus $0.1M) and the prospect and veteran age lines (23 and 29) come from my own judgement or memory and are not sourced. Treat them as placeholders. The rotation rule values come from your league chat: 240 minutes, top three at 28 or more, top two start, sub-70 players capped at 10 minutes, bench of 12. The chat did not say exactly how bench depth is counted, so I count bench players with minutes.
